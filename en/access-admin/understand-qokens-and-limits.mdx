title: "Understand Qokens and limits"
description: "Review usage and understand your organisation's soft and hard limits."
---

Qokens measure usage of the A.I. Hub's tools, helping you review usage against your organisation's limits. Open **Dashboard → Site Management → Qokens** to see the current cycle.

## Read usage

The page shows total and extra Qokens used in the current cycle, along with usage by app and team. Where included Qokens are shown, they represent the amount available to the organisation for the cycle. Extra Qokens are usage above that included amount.

Look at **Extra Qokens Usage** alongside the included-usage graph when it is shown. The page also separates usage by app and team, so you can compare where activity occurred during the cycle. The graphs and breakdowns describe usage; they do not change the limits.

The team section can also show usage adjusted for each team's share of included Qokens. Use the labels on the page to distinguish this adjusted figure from total team usage. Check the [billing page](./view-billing-and-subscription) for the current cycle dates.

## Understand the limits

The soft and hard limits are compared with **extra Qokens**, rather than total usage:

- The **soft limit** is the warning threshold for extra usage.
- The **hard limit** restricts affected requests when extra usage passes it.

For example, if the included amount is 100 Qokens and total usage is 120, the extra usage is 20. Compare that extra amount with the limits, rather than comparing the full 120 with them. A threshold is passed when extra usage is greater than the limit.

An organisation owner can edit the displayed soft and hard values using their edit controls on the Qokens page. The hard limit must be at least as high as the soft limit. Review both values before saving a change, especially if the current extra usage is already near a limit.

If a request is restricted, ask your organisation owner to review current extra usage and the hard limit. If the limit does not explain the problem, contact support with the message shown on screen.
