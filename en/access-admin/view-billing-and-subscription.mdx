---
title: "View billing and subscription"
description: "Review subscription details, invoices, and billing recovery options."
---

The billing page brings together your organisation's subscription status, cycle dates, and invoice information. Open **Dashboard → Site Management → Billing** to review the subscription type and status, current billing cycle dates, upcoming invoice details, and usage by team. When applicable, it also shows trial dates or a scheduled cancellation date.

## When to use it

Use the billing page to review the current subscription. An owner or finance manager can open Stripe from there to manage payment details or invoices. If access is interrupted and you are directed to the recovery page, follow the recovery steps below.

## Read the page

**Subscription Details** shows the subscription type, status, and current cycle start and end. Trial start and end appear when the subscription is in a trial. A **Cancellation scheduled** notice, when shown, gives the date through which the subscription remains active.

**Upcoming Invoice Details** lists products, quantities, unit prices, and totals, with a discounted price column when a discount applies. Team usage is shown separately. For payment or invoice questions, an owner or finance manager can review the details in Stripe.

## Manage billing

An organisation owner or finance manager can select **Manage billing or cancel** to open Stripe. There, you can update the payment method, download invoices, or review cancellation. Check the effective cancellation date in Stripe before confirming any change.

If you do not see the billing management button, ask an organisation owner or finance manager for help. If Stripe does not open, try again. Contact support if the problem continues.

## Restore access when billing needs attention

If you are directed to **Restore your MQO A.I. Hub access**, follow the steps shown on that page:

1. If outstanding invoices are listed, select **Pay invoice securely** for each invoice and complete the payment in Stripe.
2. Return to the recovery page. It checks the status automatically. You can also select **Check again now**.
3. If prompted to restart the subscription after the balance is clear, an organisation owner selects **Restart subscription** and follows the instructions.
4. When the page confirms the subscription is active, select **Return to Hub**.

If the page cannot check billing status, select **Try again**. If you are not an owner and the page says an owner must restart the subscription, contact your organisation's owner.
