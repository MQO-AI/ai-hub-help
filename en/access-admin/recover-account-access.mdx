---
title: "Recover account access"
description: "Reset your password or recover access to your verification methods."
---

Recover your account by choosing the route that matches what you cannot use: your password, your verification method, or your activation link.

## When to use it

Reset your password if you cannot sign in with it. Use a recovery code if you know your password but have lost access to your passkey, security key, or authenticator app. If your account is not activated, use the activation email instead.

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your account email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email. Select a security question, enter your answer, and choose and confirm a new password.
4. Select **Reset password**, then sign in with the new password.

The confirmation screen does not establish whether an account exists for the address entered. Check the inbox for the account email you use, then follow the link to the security-question and new-password form. Your new password and confirmation must match.

If you do not receive an email, check your spam folder and make sure you entered the email address used for your account. If the link is invalid or expired, request a new one. If you cannot answer a security question, ask your organisation's administrator to send you a password reset link.

## Recover your verification method

If you cannot use your passkey, security key, or authenticator app, select **I don’t have access to these methods** on the verification screen. Enter one of the recovery codes you saved during setup and follow the prompts to add a new method. Each recovery code can be used once.

If you cannot use a method or a recovery code, select **I don’t have my recovery codes** for the recovery guidance, or ask your organisation's administrator for an MFA reset. After the reset, sign in again and set up a new method. Organisation owners and people who cannot reach an administrator can contact MQO support.

A password reset does not reset your two-factor authentication. Keep your password, setup keys, verification codes, and recovery codes private.

## If your account is not activated

Open the activation link sent to your email. If the sign-in screen offers **Resend activation email**, select it and check your inbox and spam folder for the new message.
