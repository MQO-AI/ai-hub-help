---
title: "Set up two-factor authentication"
description: "Add a sign-in method, save recovery codes, and recover access."
---

Two-factor authentication adds a verification step after your password to help protect your account. Your organisation may require it and may limit which methods you can use.

## When to use it

Add a passkey or an authenticator app when prompted, or when you want an additional sign-in check. Choose an available method you can use at sign-in, and save your recovery codes in case you lose access to it.

## Add a sign-in method

1. Open **Profile**, then select **Manage account security** in **Account Security**.
2. Choose to add a sign-in method. Confirm your identity if prompted.
3. Choose an available method:
   - **Create a passkey** and follow your device or security key prompts.
   - **Authenticator app**: scan the QR code with your app, then enter the current six-digit code. You can also enter the setup key manually.
4. Save the recovery codes somewhere private. Confirm that you saved them, then enter one of the saved codes to finish. This confirmation does not use up the code.

A passkey uses the device or security key prompt, such as a screen lock or fingerprint. An authenticator app supplies a changing six-digit code. The setup screen offers a QR code and a manual setup key for the app. The recovery-code screen offers **Download codes** before you confirm that you saved them.

If you add a passkey, you can add another permitted method as a backup. Keep the backup somewhere you can access independently of the first device. Your organisation's policy determines which methods are permitted.

## Sign in with your method

After entering your email and password, choose a verification method if prompted. Follow the passkey prompt, or enter the current code from your authenticator app. If a verification expires, start sign-in again.

## If you lose access

On the verification screen, select **I don’t have access to these methods** and enter a saved recovery code. Each recovery code can be used once. Follow the prompts to set up a new method.

If you cannot use a method or recovery code, contact your organisation's administrator. After your administrator resets your two-factor authentication, sign in again and set up a new method. Organisation owners and people who cannot reach an administrator should contact MQO support.

## Manage your security

In **Account Security**, you can review your methods, recovery codes, signed-in devices, and recent security activity. You can add another method before removing an old one, review signed-in devices, and sign out a device you no longer use. Some changes require you to verify your identity again. Organisation requirements can prevent you from removing a required method.
