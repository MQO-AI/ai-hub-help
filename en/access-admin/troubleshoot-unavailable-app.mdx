---
title: "Troubleshoot an unavailable app"
description: "Check your account and access when you cannot open an A.I. Hub app."
---

When an app is missing or will not open, these checks help you identify whether the problem is with sign-in, app access, or a particular item. Your organisation administrator can help check your membership and app access.

## Check your account and organisation

1. Make sure you can sign in with the email address you use for your organisation. If you cannot sign in, follow [Recover account access](./recover-account-access).
2. If you have an invitation, check that you completed the join steps for the intended organisation. See [Sign in and join](./sign-in-and-join).
3. If your account or organisation is shown as disabled, contact your organisation administrator. An administrator or owner can check the account and subscription status.

On the sign-in screen, an email or password error calls for an account check. A request for a verification method is a separate step after the password. Use the recovery guide for the step you cannot complete.

## Check the app and the item

1. Note the name of the app and any message you see. Ask your administrator to check both the organisation's app access and your individual app assignment.
2. If the app opens but a project, file, or other item is missing, check that you are looking in the right place and ask its owner to check your access. App access alone does not establish access to every item. See [Projects and sharing across products](./projects-and-sharing-across-products).
3. If an action reports a usage limit, ask an administrator or owner to review the organisation's usage and limits. A limit affecting one action does not explain every access problem.

For example, if Content Coder opens but a project is missing, identify the project and ask its owner to check access. If the app itself is absent, an administrator can first check the organisation's app list and the person's **Change Apps** assignment.

If the app still fails, try again later and contact your administrator or support. Share the app name, the action, the visible message, and the approximate time through an approved channel. Do not send passwords, verification codes, recovery codes, or private invitation links.
