---
title: "Create an organisation and manage its registration portal"
description: "Set up an organisation and choose how people can request an account."
---

An organisation brings your users and app access together, and its registration portal gives people a way to request an account.

## When to use it

Create an organisation when you are setting up a new one. If you have been invited to an existing organisation, follow the invitation instead. See [Sign in and join](./sign-in-and-join).

Use the portal when you want to share a registration link with potential users. To invite a specific person by email, see [Invite and manage users](./invite-and-manage-users). Portal registration and app assignment are separate steps.

## Create an organisation

1. Sign in and follow the account setup prompts until you reach **Choose a name for your Organization**.
2. Enter the **Organization Name**, then select **Submit**.
3. Continue through the subscription setup shown on screen. Review the choices before confirming them.

If you see a message that an organisation is already registered for your email domain, check with your administrator before continuing. If creation fails, check the name and contact your administrator or support if the problem continues.

## Activate a registration portal

Organisation administrators can use a portal to let people register for an account. Decide which email domains, if any, should be pre-approved before sharing the link.

1. Open **Dashboard → Users → Portal** and select **Activate Portal**.
2. Enter the final part of the **Portal URL**.
3. Optionally select **Add Domain** and enter each **Pre-Approved Domains** entry, such as `example.com`. Leave the list empty to require administrator approval for all new registrations.
4. Select **Submit**. Once the portal is active, copy the **Portal URL** and share it with the intended applicants.

The URL field has a fixed prefix and a part you choose. The full address appears on the portal page after activation. Each domain has its own field: use **Add Domain** for another, or the remove control beside an entry to remove it. Enter the domain without a person's name or `@`, for example `example.com`.

A person using the portal completes the registration form and follows the email verification instructions. A registration from a pre-approved domain does not require administrator approval. Other applicants are prompted for a note to the administrator and need approval. App assignment is a separate access check.

## Review requests and maintain the portal

Open **Dashboard → Users → Invitations** and review **User Approval Requests**. The table shows the applicant’s name, email, request date, and status. Select **View Note** to read a note when one was provided. For a pending request, select **Accept** or **Reject**, then confirm your choice. Check the person's status and app access before they begin work.

On **Users → Portal**, select **Edit Portal** to change the URL or domains, then select **Submit**. Select **Disable Portal** and confirm **Disable** to stop using the portal. Give applicants the current URL after a change.

If a portal link does not open, ask an administrator to check that the portal is active and that the link is current. If registration requires approval, ask the administrator to review the request. An existing account holder should contact the administrator for the appropriate way to join.
