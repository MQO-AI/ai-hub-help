---
title: "Sign in and join your organisation"
description: "Accept an invitation, sign in, and get help if you cannot join."
---

Accept your organisation's invitation to join the A.I. Hub and start using its assigned apps. Open the invitation sent to the email address you will use for your account.

## When to use it

Follow the new-user steps if you do not yet have an A.I. Hub account. If you already have an account for the invited email address, sign in first and follow the existing-user steps.

## If you are new to the A.I. Hub

1. Open the invitation link and check the organisation name.
2. Enter your name and choose a password. Complete the security questions and accept the terms of use.
3. Select **Register**.
4. Check your email and follow the verification link. Then sign in with your email and password. If prompted for an additional verification step, see [Two-factor authentication](./two-factor-authentication).

The form displays the invited email address and organisation. Your security questions and answers are used for password recovery, so choose answers you can recall. After registration, the email verification step is separate from entering the registration form.

## If you already have an account

1. Sign in using the email address that received the invitation.
2. Open the invitation link again if needed. Check the organisation name.
3. Enter your password, accept the terms of use, and select **Join the organization**. You can also decline the invitation.

The existing-user form shows the organisation and invited email so you can confirm you are joining with the intended account. Accepting joins that organisation; declining does not complete the join.

## If you cannot join

- If the invitation is invalid or expired, ask your administrator for a new invitation.
- If you are asked to sign in first, check that you are using the invited email address.
- If you registered but cannot sign in, check your email for the account verification link.
- If you cannot remember your password, select **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen. Follow the reset email and answer the security question when prompted.
- If you can sign in but cannot open an app, ask your administrator to check your organisation membership and app access.

Keep your password, verification codes, and recovery codes private. MQO support and your administrator do not need these codes to investigate an access problem.
