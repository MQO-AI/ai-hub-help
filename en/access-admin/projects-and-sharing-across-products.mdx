---
title: "Understand projects and sharing across products"
description: "Find the right work and check access in Content Coder, Chat, Content Centre, and Asker."
---

Projects, document workspaces, and resources hold different kinds of work across A.I. Hub products. Identify the product and item your collaborator needs before sharing, because the item may have access requirements in addition to app access.

## When to use it

Use this guide when you need to find or share work across products. For example, a Content Coder project and an Asker project are separate items, even if they relate to the same survey.

## Find the work

- An **A.I. Content Coder project** holds responses and coding work. Open it in Content Coder. You can export a question or project and review the resulting file before sharing it. See [Export coding](../workflows/export-coding).
- A **Document Workspace** is linked to a Chat and holds document review and draft history. Create a draft and download it when you need a file to share. See [Work with a document](../chat-content/work-with-a-document).
- An **Asker project** is work in Asker. Check access in Asker for that project and its associated items. A Survey transfer to Content Coder is a separate send and import action. See [Import Survey transfers](../workflows/import-survey-transfers).
- A Content Centre **Resource** is saved material that can be reused. Its **Shared with** setting lets you choose an audience. A **Collection** groups Resources. Review access to the material a recipient needs. See [Save and organise resources](../chat-content/save-and-organise-resources).

In Asker, **Projects** brings together linked research work. Its project view can list Surveys, Registrations, Focus Groups, Workshops, and Sessions workspaces separately. A Survey or Registration can also be used without a project. Linking one to a project helps organise it; the association does not itself enrol people, send invitations, or grant access.

Creating a project, saving a draft, or starting a chat does not by itself share all related work with another person. Check team membership, app access, and the item's sharing settings separately.

## If someone cannot find the work

1. Confirm the product, item name, and account the person is using. For example, ask whether they need the Asker Survey, the imported Content Coder project, or a downloaded export.
2. Ask an administrator to check the person's app access. Ask the item owner to check the relevant sharing or access settings.
3. For a transferred Survey, check that it was sent and imported. For a shared file, check that the intended file was sent to the recipient.

If the item is still unavailable, contact your administrator or support with the product, action, and visible message. Avoid sending private content or access links as part of the initial report.
